GRADE 3 ENGLISH & GRAMMAR LESSON PLAN
=========================================
CHAPTER 2: Action Verbs and Storytelling
Target Grade: Grade 3
Duration: 45 - 60 Minutes

[SECTION 1: OVERVIEW & OBJECTIVES]
-----------------------------------------
* Lesson Topic: Identifying and using action verbs to describe movement.
* Grammar Focus: Subject-Verb Agreement (Singular vs. Plural Actions).
* Learning Objectives:
  1. Students will be able to identify action verbs in a sentence.
  2. Students will understand how verbs change when a subject is singular or plural.
  3. Students will apply action verbs to write a three-sentence mini-story.

[SECTION 2: WARM-UP ACTIVITY (5 MINS)]
-----------------------------------------
* Activity Name: "Simon Says... Actions Only!"
* Instruction: The teacher acts out a movement without speaking (e.g., jumping, clapping, writing). 
* Prompt: "Class, look at what I am doing. What is the word for this action? Raise your hand if you can tell me!"
* Goal: Get students thinking about movement words (verbs).

[SECTION 3: INSTRUCTIONAL TEXT - WHAT IS A VERB? (15 MINS)]
-----------------------------------------
* Lesson Content:
  Nouns are the names of objects or people, but verbs are the ACTION words! Verbs tell us what a noun is doing. Without verbs, nothing moves in our writing!
  
  Examples of Action Verbs:
  - The dog BARKED at the mail carrier.
  - Maya RUNS fast during recess.
  - We READ a funny book together.

* Interactive Check:
  "Look at this sentence: 'The creative student drew a beautiful picture.' 
  Which word tells us what the student DID? (Answer: drew)"

[SECTION 4: GRAMMAR RULES - SUBJECT-VERB AGREEMENT (15 MINS)]
-----------------------------------------
* Lesson Content:
  Verbs have a special rule when matching with nouns in the present tense:
  
  Rule 1: If ONE person or thing is doing the action (Singular), the verb usually needs an "-s" or "-es" at the end.
  - Example: One bird CHIRPS in the tree.
  
  Rule 2: If MORE THAN ONE person or thing is doing the action (Plural), the verb does NOT need an "-s".
  - Example: Two birds CHIRP in the tree.

* Guided Practice:
  Fill in the blank with the correct verb form:
  1. The excited puppy ______ (chase / chases) the ball. 
  2. Three children ______ (laugh / laughs) at the joke.
  
  *Answer Key: 1) chases, 2) laugh*

[SECTION 5: INDEPENDENT PRACTICE & ASSESSMENT (10 MINS)]
-----------------------------------------
* Creative Writing Assignment:
  "Write a short 3-sentence story about an animal of your choice. You must include at least THREE action verbs. Underline your action verbs so your teacher can see them!"

* Exit Ticket Worksheet:
  Question 1: Circle the action verb: "Leo swims in the deep pool."
  Question 2: Correct this sentence: "The two frogs jumps into the pond."
  
  *Answer Key for Exit Ticket:*
  1) swims
  2) "The two frogs jump into the pond." (Remove the 's' because frogs is plural).
